Why we exist

A counterfeit pack is not a small problem.

It is a farmer who loses a season to spurious seed. A patient taking an inert tablet. A parent buying fake baby food. A brand whose reputation is destroyed by a product it never made.

The technology to prevent most of this already exists — it was just built for the West's largest pharmaceutical exporters and priced beyond everyone else. We started VeriTrace AI to change that: enterprise-grade serialization that an MSME in Rajkot or Coimbatore can actually adopt, in their language, at their budget, under Indian law.

Protect the customer first

Verification must work for a first-time smartphone user in a low-signal village. If the consumer cannot use it, the security model is decoration.

Respect the operator

Software that fights a shop floor loses. We design for gloved hands, noisy rooms and shift targets — not for a demo on a laptop.

Earn the trust we sell

We ask manufacturers to put their brand behind our codes. So our own security, privacy and residency standards are non-negotiable.

Our story

Built alongside manufacturers, not at them.

VeriTrace began on a factory floor in Gujarat, watching a line supervisor attempt to explain a print fault to an engineer over a phone call. The product we build today is a direct answer to that afternoon.

2023

The first line

Our first pilot ran on a single filling line in Rajkot. It printed 40,000 codes in a week and found a print-head problem the plant had been living with for months.

2024

Aggregation and the operator assistant

Carton and pallet hierarchy shipped, along with the first version of the assistant in Hindi and Marathi — after operators told us the manual was useless at 2am on a night shift.

2025

Consumer rewards at scale

UPI-linked cashback and warranty registration went live. Brands discovered something unexpected: verification scans were the most honest demand signal they had ever had.

2026

The intelligence layer

Anomaly detection and the document assistant turn a stream of scans into plain-language action — and turn a three-week audit scramble into an afternoon's export.

How we work

Four commitments we do not negotiate.

01

India data residency

Serialization and consumer data live in Indian data centres by default. Not as an enterprise add-on — as the starting position.

02

DPDP-aligned by design

Consent, purpose limitation and erasure are built into the consumer flows, not bolted on by a legal review afterwards.

03

Your data is not our product

We do not train shared models on your operational data, and we do not sell scan data to third parties. Ever.

04

Honest engineering

If your line does not need a new printer, we say so. If a pilot did not hit target, you get the real numbers.
