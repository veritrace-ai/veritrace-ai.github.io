There are currently no open incidents. The most recent resolved incident is listed below.

Duration: 41 minutes · Impact: Analytics dashboards slow, serialization unaffected

Root cause. An index rebuild on the scan-events table coincided with an unusually large nightly batch from a high-volume customer, saturating IO on the analytics replica.

What we changed. Index rebuilds are now rate-limited and excluded from peak ingestion windows. We also added IO saturation alerting with a five-minute warning threshold, and moved the two largest tenants to dedicated analytics replicas.

Serialization, verification and consumer pages were not affected. No customer data was lost.

Duration: 1 hour 12 minutes · Impact: Delayed webhook delivery to ERP integrations

Root cause. A downstream ERP endpoint at a single customer began responding slowly, exhausting a shared delivery worker pool. The backpressure was not isolated per tenant.

What we changed. Webhook delivery pools are now isolated per tenant with circuit breakers, so a slow endpoint at one customer cannot degrade delivery for another. We also exposed per-integration delivery lag in the console.

Scan ingestion and verification were unaffected throughout.
