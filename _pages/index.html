How it works

Five steps from factory floor to consumer trust.

Scroll to explore
Step 01

Serialize

Generate cryptographically unique IDs and QR codes at line speed — up to 1,200 units per minute, with configurable schemas per SKU, batch or market.

Step 02

Print & apply

Direct integration with TTO, laser, inkjet and label applicators. Print-head health and verification cameras catch missing or unreadable codes before they leave the line.

Step 03

Aggregate

Build the parent-child hierarchy — unit to carton to pallet to shipment. One scan at dispatch reconciles thousands of child codes with a verifiable, auditable trail.

Step 04

Trace

Every scan at every hop is recorded with time, place and actor. See live inventory, movement exceptions, and exactly where a consignment paused.

Step 05

Engage

Consumers scan to verify authenticity and unlock rewards, cashback, warranties or refills — while you capture first-party data and real demand signals.

01 — Production

The code is born with the product.

A unique, encrypted identifier is generated and printed at the moment of filling. Nothing is pre-printed, nothing is reused, nothing is guessable.

02 — Aggregation

Every unit knows its parents.

Units are linked to cartons, cartons to pallets. One scan at the pallet level reconciles thousands of child codes — with a verifiable audit trail.

03 — Dispatch

Chain of custody, captured automatically.

Gate scans, e-way bills and GST invoices attach to the shipment. If a consignment is split or diverted, you see it the moment it happens.

04 — Retail

Know what is actually on the shelf.

Stock age, outlet-level availability and movement velocity — the ground truth that distributors' spreadsheets never give you.

05 — Consumer

Trust, rewarded.

The consumer scans, sees the full journey, and is rewarded for checking. You get a verified purchase signal and a direct relationship.
