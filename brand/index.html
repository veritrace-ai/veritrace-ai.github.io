Clear space & minimum size

Clear space = the height of the shield on all sides. Never place type or edges inside it.

  • Minimum symbol size: 20 px / 5 mm
  • Minimum lockup width: 120 px / 30 mm
  • Preferred backgrounds: white, #F6F9FC, or deep blue
  • Never: stretch, rotate, add shadows, outline, or recolour outside the palette
