Why VeriTrace AI

Six capabilities. One trust layer.

02

Traceability & aggregation

03

Central multi-plant control

One login for every plant, line and warehouse. Allocate code volumes per site, change roles in a click, and watch every floor on a single screen.

04

AI intelligence layer

A digital twin per unit, a copilot that answers in plain language, predictive recall simulation and live anomaly detection — insight your team acts on, not dashboards nobody reads.

05

Consumer engagement

How it works

Five steps from factory floor to consumer trust.

Scroll to explore
Step 01

Serialize

Generate cryptographically unique IDs and QR codes at line speed — up to 1,200 units per minute, with configurable schemas per SKU, batch or market.

Step 02

Print & apply

Direct integration with TTO, laser, inkjet and label applicators. Print-head health and verification cameras catch missing or unreadable codes before they leave the line.

Step 03

Aggregate

Build the parent-child hierarchy — unit to carton to pallet to shipment. One scan at dispatch reconciles thousands of child codes with a verifiable, auditable trail.

Step 04

Trace

Every scan at every hop is recorded with time, place and actor. See live inventory, movement exceptions, and exactly where a consignment paused.

Step 05

Engage

Consumers scan to verify authenticity and unlock rewards, cashback, warranties or refills — while you capture first-party data and real demand signals.

Built to survive

If a line goes dark, the AI finishes the job.

Operators forget to sync. Networks drop mid-shift. Disks fail. VeriTrace AI assumes all three will happen and builds around them, so a bad afternoon never becomes a lost batch.

Auto-sync when the operator misses it

If a shift ends without a sync, an AI agent picks up the pending batch and completes it to the central platform — with a timestamped record of exactly what it reconciled, and what it could not.

AI-run backups to central

Each plant’s code pool, scan log and aggregation tree is continuously replicated to the central platform. No manual export, no overnight job to forget, no single disk holding your only copy.

Crash recovery, not start-over

If a plant system fails, the console restores it from the last verified state and replays every offline scan. Production resumes without reprinting a single code or re-running a batch.

Intelligence layer

Most traceability tools store events. Ours reads them.

Serialization is table stakes. What separates VeriTrace AI is what happens to the data afterwards — the models that watch the stream, predict what comes next, and tell your team what to do about it.

01

A digital twin for every unit

Not just a code — a living record holding that unit’s batch, journey, scan history and condition. Trace a complaint back to the exact pallet, line and shift in one query.

02

A copilot that speaks your language

Ask “which batches are at diversion risk this week?” and get a straight answer with the evidence behind it — in English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or Telugu.

03

Predictive recall simulation

Model a recall before you commit to one. See which batches, distributors and customers are affected, and how far the impact reaches — in seconds rather than days.

04

Vision-based print quality

Computer vision grades every printed code on the line, spots print-head drift before rejection rates climb, and stops a bad run at the point of failure — not at the customer.

05

Anomaly and diversion detection

Models watch the scan stream continuously for bulk scans at one pin code, impossible geographies and sudden return spikes — surfacing each as a plain-language action with an owner.

06

Guidance that lands with operators

Rather than burying people in dashboards, the system highlights the one thing that needs attention right now — with the fix, the reason and who to escalate to, on the line and in their language.
